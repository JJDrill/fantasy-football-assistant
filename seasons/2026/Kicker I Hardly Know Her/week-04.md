in Wk3, and Stevenson has lost his job. The best RB target is **Tony Pollard (Russini's bench)**, the
TEN lead back (17-74 rushing, 4-22 receiving in Wk3,

missing Collins (O) and A.J. Brown (IR) and starts Vele at WR, so a WR is the currency. Pitch
**McMillan ↔ Pollard**, or Stevenson + Likely ↔ Pollard as a lower-cost opener. Note that Russini's is
this week's opponent, so anything traded plays for them against you on Sunday. Run `trade-analyzer`
before sending.
