# Waiver Log

Every add we make (waiver claim or free agent, DEF and K streams included), graded after the week by `post-game-check`. League-wide
start/sit grading (`league-decisions.md`) can't see roster moves, so this is the evidence for our waiver process. Started 2026-10-24;
the first week logged is Wk7.

**Columns:** Wk = the week the add was for. ECR = his positional FantasyPros rank when we added him. Pts = his points that week
(Excl. TDs = −6 per rushing/receiving TD). Dropped = who we let go and his points that week (on any roster, or on the wire). Best
available = the top scorer at that position who was a free agent when we added, if known. Grade = process / outcome, as in
`post-game-check` Step 5. Since = for adds still rostered: weeks started and points per start, updated weekly.
