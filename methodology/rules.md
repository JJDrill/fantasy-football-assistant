# Standing Rules (all seasons)

Rules that should hold in any season and any league, because they rest on how fantasy football works, not on this year's players.
This season's specific suggestions live in each season's `reference/season-lessons.md` ("This season's rules").

**Confidence:**
- **Structural:** true by logic (e.g. a player on bye scores 0). Keep unless the league format changes.
- **Provisional:** backed by one season's data. Re-check against next season's data before treating it as proven.
- **Proven:** has held across 2+ seasons.

**How a rule gets here:** `post-game-check` proposes rule changes and says whether each one is all-seasons or this-season
(Step 9). A this-season rule is promoted here when it would make sense with a different roster and league, and the evidence isn't
one team's quirk. A rule here that next season's data contradicts goes back to "provisional" or is removed, with a dated note.

## Lineup

1. **Check every starter for bye, Out/Doubtful and IR before each lineup lock.** *Structural.* A bye or inactive starter is a
   guaranteed 0, and it's the most common avoidable mistake. Wk7 2026: 5 such starters across 2 of 9 other teams in one bye week,
   vs. 1 in 40 team-weeks over Wks 1-4. Bye weeks are when other managers leak the most points.
2. **Re-rank the whole position group each week, bench included.** *Structural.* Never frame it as only "current starter vs. the
   new option." 2026: Likely sat for weeks because every TE call was "Pitts vs. the new guy" (`season-lessons.md`).
3. **Expert consensus (FantasyPros ECR) first, with Vegas totals, then tiebreakers.** *Provisional (2026).* League-wide replay of
   Wks 1-4: ECR-first was +36.6 points and 4 extra wins over 40 team-weeks; usage-first was −155 over 30.
   - **WR:** don't override ECR except for a clear role change it hasn't caught (overriding was wrong 7 of 10 times).
   - **Inside a narrow rank gap it's a coin flip:** break it with usage or role change, then health. 2026 starting values:
     TE ~5 spots, RB ~3, FLEX ~10 (start the later kickoff). Recalibrate the window sizes each season.
   - Usage can't see a player returning from injury; don't let it override ECR for him.
4. **Don't use defense-vs-position matchup grades.** *Provisional (2026).* They hit 33% (7/21) in 2026, and the matchup is already
   priced into ECR and Vegas totals.
5. **A Q tag plus a low implied team total is a tiebreaker against the player** when the ranks are close. Prefer the healthy,
   steady-volume player. *Provisional (2026).*

## Judging our own decisions

6. **Grade process separately from outcome.** *Structural.* A right call can lose to luck, and a wrong call can get away with it.
   One week's bad-luck result is never evidence for changing a rule.
7. **Judge the team on points scored, not the record.** *Structural.* Close games are mostly luck. A losing record by itself is
   never a reason for panic moves or a rebuild. 2026: we were 2-4 while 4th in points; Gospel was 0-6 on median scoring.
8. **Discount touchdowns when grading a call.** *Structural.* TDs are the noisiest part of a score. Judge whether a source picked
   the player who got the role and production (`source-accuracy.md` scores calls with and without TDs).
9. **Accept as random:** kicker swings, an uncleared backup booming, a low-usage WR spiking from nowhere, opponent ceiling weeks.
   *Structural.* Don't change rules to chase these.

## Roster and waivers

10. **Don't drop a player with a real role after one bad week.** *Provisional (2026).* Stevenson (dropped Sep 30) and Marks
    (dropped Oct 11) both came back to hurt us.
11. **Byes are planned a week ahead:** when to add the cover, and who to drop. *Structural.*

## Trades

12. **Read league-wide supply and demand before pitching.** *Structural.* A position many teams have spare (2026: QB) has few
    buyers, and each buyer disappears once someone else sells to them. Pitch a scarce position's buyers promptly. Scouting every
    team each week (`team-scout`) is how we see the market.
13. **Manager type predicts who will deal.** *Provisional (2026).* Inactive and set-and-forget managers rarely answer offers; aim
    pitches at active managers, and only send a passive one an offer that's clearly good for them.

## Sources

14. **Tier sources before believing them** (`methodology/trusted-sources.md`). *Structural.* Rumors get logged as Unverified,
    never acted on.

## Platform (Yahoo)

15. **Yahoo won't let you re-add a player you dropped until he clears waivers** (Error #245). Weigh that before dropping a handcuff.
    *Structural.*
16. **An IR-slotted player whose tag goes back to Q blocks every add** until he's moved to the bench (Error #845). *Structural.*
